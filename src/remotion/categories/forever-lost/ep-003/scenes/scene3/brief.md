# Scene 3 — Physically Inaccessible (Radiation)

---

## 1. Voice Script

### 🎭 Style Instructions (System Prompt for TTS)

Voice: Charon
Style: Grim, factual narrator. Almost cold. The information is shocking enough without drama.
Pacing: Slightly faster than previous scenes — building urgency and frustration.
Tone: Every attempt to get close to the corium has failed. There should be an undertone of hopelessness.
Emphasis: "Three hundred seconds", "AK-47", "robots", "self-shielding"

### 📝 The Annotated Script (User Prompt for TTS)

[Factual, cold, building.]
In December 1986, eight months after the explosion, workers finally reached the basement of Reactor Four.
[pause 1s]
They found the Elephant's Foot. A Geiger counter held nearby registered... thirty-eight thousand roentgens per hour.
[pause 1.2s]
[Emphasis]
At that dose, a human being receives a lethal amount of radiation in thirty seconds.
[pause 1.5s]

[Facts, building pace]
Today, the radiation has decayed. But only to about one percent of its original intensity.
[pause 1s]
That still means three hundred seconds — five minutes — is a fatal dose.
[pause 1.5s]

[Frustrated tone]
You cannot send a human near it. So you send a robot.
[pause 1s]
The radiation fries the robot's electronics in minutes. The cameras go white. The circuits fail. The robot becomes a melted corpse next to the corium.
[pause 1.5s]

[Slightly darker]
When scientists needed a sample of the Elephant's Foot to study it, they couldn't get close enough to use a drill.
[pause 1.2s]
They mounted a rifle round on a long pole, fired an AK-47 from a distance, and chipped off fragments.
[pause 1.5s]

[Slow, heavy]
And this is the problem.
[pause 1.5s]
The corium is self-shielding. Its own mass blocks its radiation... which means the inner core of the flow is still far hotter than the outside.
[pause 1s]
Even the act of trying to cut or move it would release lethal doses of radiation into the environment.
[pause 1.5s]
We cannot touch it. We cannot cut it. We cannot lift it.
[pause 2s]
