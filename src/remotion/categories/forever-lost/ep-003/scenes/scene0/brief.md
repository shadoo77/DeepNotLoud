# Scene 0 — The Elephant's Foot (Hook)

---

## 1. Voice Script

### 🎭 Style Instructions (System Prompt for TTS)

Voice: Charon
Style: Deep, measured documentary narrator. Intimate, foreboding, but not sensational.
Pacing: Very slow. Let every sentence land. Pause between clauses.
Tone: You are describing the most dangerous object ever created. No shouting — the material speaks for itself.
Emphasis: Lean on "five minutes" and "never be removed." The final sentence drops in pitch.

### 📝 The Annotated Script (User Prompt for TTS)

[Solemn, intimate tone. Very slow pacing.]
Somewhere beneath a collapsed building in northern Ukraine...
[pause 1.5s]
there is a photograph.
[pause 1.5s]
It looks like tree bark. Or industrial sludge. Maybe a pile of coal.
[pause 1.2s]
But what you are actually looking at...
[pause 1s]
is the most dangerous object on Earth.
[pause 1.5s]
It is called the Elephant's Foot.
[pause 1s]
A mass of molten nuclear fuel, concrete, steel, and sand — fused together at twenty-six hundred degrees Celsius.
[pause 1.5s]
In 1986, thirty seconds of exposure meant certain death.
[pause 1.2s]
Today... five minutes will kill you.
[pause 2s]
[Drop pitch, cold finality]
And it can never, ever be removed.
[pause 2s]
