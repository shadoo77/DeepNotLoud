## 1. Voice Script

### 🎭 Style Instructions (System Prompt for TTS)

Voice: Charon
Style: Documentary narrator, factual but grave. Like a historian recounting a catastrophe.
Pacing: Steady, building slightly. Use micro-pauses between stats so each number lands.
Tone: Cold facts, but the facts themselves are horrifying. Don't over-emote — let the numbers terrify.
Emphasis: The doses, the evacuees, the square kilometers. "Four hundred times" needs weight.

### 📝 The Annotated Script (User Prompt for TTS)

[Neutral, factual tone. Steady pace.]
April 26th, 1986. One twenty-three in the morning.
[pause 1s]
Reactor Four at the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant — an RBMK design, known to be unstable at low power — was running a safety test.
[pause 1.2s]
A power surge. A control rod design flaw. A reactor with a positive void coefficient that made it inherently dangerous.
[pause 1.5s]
[Slow down, emphasis]
In four seconds... the reactor's power output spiked to one hundred times its normal maximum.
[pause 1.5s]
The uranium fuel rods fractured. Cooling water flashed to steam. The steam pressure blew the thousand-ton biological shield clean through the roof.
[pause 1.5s]
And then...
[pause 1s]
the second explosion tore the building apart.
[pause 2s]

[Measured, building]
Fifty tons of radioactive material were thrown into the atmosphere — four hundred times more than Hiroshima.
[pause 1s]
One hundred sixty thousand square kilometers of Europe contaminated.
[pause 1s]
Three hundred fifty thousand people permanently evacuated from their homes.
[pause 1.5s]
[Drop slightly]
And deep inside what remained of Reactor Four...
[pause 1.5s]
two hundred tons of nuclear fuel... had begun to melt.
[pause 2s]
