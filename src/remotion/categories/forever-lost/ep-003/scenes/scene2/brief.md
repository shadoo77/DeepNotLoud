# Scene 2 — What the Core Became (Corium / Lava)

---

## 1. Voice Script

### 🎭 Style Instructions (System Prompt for TTS)

Voice: Charon
Style: Scientific yet awe-struck documentary narrator. Explaining something almost supernatural.
Pacing: Deliberate, with wonder. Allow the absurdity of what happened to sink in.
Tone: The material created is unlike anything else on Earth. Let that awe — and horror — come through.
Emphasis: "Lava", "twenty-six hundred", "three different flows", "only the tip"

### 📝 The Annotated Script (User Prompt for TTS)

[Scientific, measured, but with quiet awe.]
When a nuclear reactor melts down, the fuel doesn't just stop being fuel.
[pause 1s]
It becomes something else.
[pause 1.5s]

[A bit faster, explaining]
Inside Reactor Four, the two hundred tons of uranium fuel rods reached twenty-six hundred degrees Celsius — hot enough to melt steel, concrete, and sand. Hot enough to burn through solid floors.
[pause 1.5s]
The fuel mixed with the reactor's graphite moderator, the zirconium cladding from the rods, the concrete from the biological shield, and the sand used to surround the core for insulation.
[pause 1.5s]

[Slower, emphasizing]
The result was a new material. Scientists call it corium — lava-like fuel-containing material.
[pause 1.2s]
It flowed. Down through the reactor vessel, through concrete floors, through service corridors. Three separate flows, each one a river of molten nuclear death.
[pause 2s]

[Darker tone]
The most famous of these flows — the one everyone calls the Elephant's Foot — is just one mass. It sits in a basement corridor.
[pause 1.2s]
But the rest of the corium... is still buried. Still hot. Still radiating. Still slowly burning its way through the foundations of the building.
[pause 1.5s]
And no one can get to it.
[pause 2s]
