# Scene 5 — A 20,000-Year Grave (Outro)

---

## 1. Voice Script

### 🎭 Style Instructions (System Prompt for TTS)

Voice: Charon
Style: Quiet, philosophical, close. This is the ending. The tone should settle into something almost peaceful — acceptance of the inevitable.
Pacing: Very slow. Long pauses. Let silence be part of the delivery.
Tone: Not sad. Not angry. Just... accepting. Some things are beyond us.
Emphasis: "Twenty thousand years", "walk away", "forever lost" — the final line hangs.

### 📝 The Annotated Script (User Prompt for TTS)

[Quiet. Philosophical. Slow.]
So the core of Chernobyl's Reactor Four remains exactly where it fell.
[pause 2s]
Buried under concrete and steel. Entombed inside a billion-dollar arch. Slowly cooling. Slowly decaying.
[pause 2.5s]

[Measured, factual]
The corium will remain dangerous for at least twenty thousand years. Maybe longer.
[pause 2s]
We have built structures to contain it... but no structure lasts twenty thousand years.
[pause 2.5s]

[Soft, accepting]
The Egyptians built the pyramids forty-five hundred years ago, and they are crumbling.
[pause 2s]
The oldest wooden structures on Earth are about seven thousand years old.
[pause 1.5s]
Twenty thousand years... is twice as long as all of recorded human history.
[pause 3s]

[Slow, emotional close]
Every civilization that has ever existed — every empire, every war, every invention, every life — all of it fits inside that span.
[pause 2s]
The core will still be here, long after everything we know has turned to dust.
[pause 2.5s]
[Very quiet, final]
Some things are so dangerous...
[pause 2s]
so impossible...
[pause 2s]
that the only solution is to walk away.
[pause 3s]
Forever lost.
[pause 3s]
