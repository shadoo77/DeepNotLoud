# FOREVER LOST — Episode: "The Real Loser in Every World Cup"
### ⚡ SHORT FORMAT — TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts
> Target Duration: 50–60 seconds | Format: 9:16 Portrait | FPS: 30 | Language: English

---

## ⚡ SHORT FORMAT PHILOSOPHY

This is NOT a summary of the long video.
It's a **standalone gut-punch** — one hook, five blows, one closer.
Each World Cup gets ONE sentence. ONE stat. ONE motion beat.
The viewer should feel breathless by the end.

> **PACING RULE:** 3 seconds per beat. No exceptions. Every cut is a hit.
