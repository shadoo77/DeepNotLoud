## WHY THE SCRIPTS WERE REWRITTEN

Charon delivers at roughly **8–9 words per second** with gravelly tone — even with pauses removed.
The previous scripts had ~254 words across 7 scenes → ~155s.
Target is 60s → **maximum ~77 words total across all scenes**.

Every word below was chosen because it cannot be cut.
No sentences. No explanations. Just facts that hit like a fist.
